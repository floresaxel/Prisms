The 20 names

Crowding is my offline gut-check of how contested each name feels (similar products, famous marks) — it is not a trademark or domain search. Verify before falling in love.

Light & Optics

Continuity picks — keep the Prisms heritage, sharpen the metaphor.

Refract

Prisms as a verb. One white beam of “everything I have to do” goes in; separate, ordered colors come out. The name does what the app does.

Flatters: My Day — one day, split into focus.

Crowding: low–meda few small dev tools, nothing dominant

Facet

One life, many faces: tasks, journal, habits, projects are facets of the same stone — and this is where they meet. Quietly premium, easy to say in any language.

Flatters: the grouped sidebar — every area one facet.

Crowding: mediumcommon word; a wealth-mgmt firm uses it

Spectra

What a prism produces: the full range of your life laid out in order, nothing blended into mud. The plural feels like a system, not a tool.

Flatters: Dashboard + Projects — the whole range at a glance.

Crowding: mediumscattered brands across industries

Lumen

The unit of light output. A day well-run has a brightness to it — this is where you generate it. Short, latinate, timeless.

Flatters: Habits & streaks — output you can measure.

Crowding: hightelecom giant, metabolism device, PHP framework

Aperture

The mechanism that decides how much light gets in. Attention as a dial you set, not an accident that happens to you.

Flatters: the global focus timer.

Crowding: mediumApple’s retired photo app; Portal’s lab is nerd-charm, not a competitor

Time & Rhythm

The system that keeps you ticking — daily, weekly, seasonal.

Cadence

The rhythm a good week settles into: plan, do, review, repeat. Musical and human where most productivity words are cold.

Flatters: Review + Habits — the weekly beat.

Crowding: med–highEDA giant (far field) + several calendar startups

Meridian

The line the sun crosses at noon — time and place in one word. A reference line for the day; everything is measured from it.

Flatters: Agenda — the day has a spine.

Crowding: mediumbanks & audio brands use it; none in this space

Sundial

The first instrument that turned light into a plan for the day — which is literally the product. Concrete, friendly, spellable by ear.

Flatters: My Day + Agenda; bridges the Prisms light-heritage.

Crowding: low–meda small analytics startup used it

Vesper

The evening star — and the hour when the day gets written down and closed. Elegant, a little moody; owns the nightly ritual.

Flatters: Journal + evening review.

Crowding: mediumthe cocktail, the Bond character, and a beloved-but-retired iOS notes app

Mainspring

The coiled spring that powers a watch all week from one winding. Your system as the thing that drives the days — and a wink at the wearables roadmap.

Flatters: Automations + the watch plans.

Crowding: lowclear in this space

Journey & Structure

Progress you can point at; structure that holds you steady.

Waypoint

A route is just a chain of small fixed points. Plans you can actually hit, one mark at a time.

Flatters: Tasks & substeps — every step a point on the route.

Crowding: mediumHashiCorp’s (discontinued) deploy tool — devs know it

Cairn / KAIRN /

The stone stacks that mark a trail — built one rock at a time. Streaks and progress made physical; you can see how far you’ve come.

Flatters: Habits — one stone a day.

Crowding: low–medan outdoor-gear subscription box

Keel

The part of the boat you never see that keeps the whole thing steady — and lets you sail against the wind. “On an even keel” is the brand promise.

Flatters: the whole system — calm-productivity positioning.

Crowding: lowonly a tiny k8s tool shares it

Trellis

Structure that doesn’t force growth — it just gives it something to climb. The kindest possible metaphor for routines.

Flatters: Habits + Projects — scaffolding, not pressure.

Crowding: low–meda WordPress dev tool shares it

Strata

Days deposit into layers: task → project → area → life. Geological patience — your record accumulates into something solid.

Flatters: Journal + Review + Dashboard — the accumulated record.

Crowding: mediuma retired O’Reilly conference; real-estate term in AU/NZ

Paper & Ink

Journal-forward — the writing desk as the center of gravity.

Daybook

The historical name for the ledger you wrote each day’s business in before sorting it — which is exactly My Day + Journal + Review. Needs zero explanation.

Flatters: Journal + My Day, in one old word.

Crowding: med–higha journaling app already trades under it on Android

Folio

A leaf of a manuscript; a life kept in well-ordered pages. Compact, bookish, premium.

Flatters: Journal + the per-day .md export.

Crowding: mediumassorted fintech “folios”

Almanac

The book that holds a year’s rhythms — what’s coming, what recurs, what the season demands. Planning, recurrence and reflection in one word.

Flatters: Agenda + Review — the long rhythm.

Crowding: mediuma VC-backed docs startup used it

Craft & Practice

The daily discipline, and the place it all comes home to.

Kata / KAH-tah /

The practiced form you repeat until it’s second nature. Habits and automations as craft, not willpower. Four letters, pronounceable everywhere.

Flatters: Habits + Automations.

Crowding: medium“code kata” culture + Kata Containers — arguably a feature for this audience

Harbor

The home base every day departs from and returns to. Not about doing more — about having somewhere everything is held. Calm positioning in one word.

Flatters: the shell itself — the place you keep coming back to.

Crowding: med–highCNCF’s container registry — every dev knows it

The 100 brand styles

Each is a full direction — palette, type, shape language, voice. The wordmark and the mini “My Day” card render whichever name you clicked above (default: Prisms). Roughly what the topbar brand and a landing hero would feel like. Styles 01–07 are the core, app-adjacent directions; 08–27 range wider — print, dark, retro, municipal; 28–47 are the color round — jewel tones, liveries, tiles, citrus, neon; 48–59 go multi-color + geometric — triangle meshes, prism outlines, tangrams, tessellations, all kept subtle; 60–100 are the deep cuts — materials, tickets, instruments, boards and places — all the way to a round hundred. Some go dark or loud on purpose; none touches a purple-pink gradient.
