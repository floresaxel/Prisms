Structure

Visions become roadmaps.
Roadmaps become Tuesdays.

Everything you plan hangs off one tree — Vision → Roadmap → Project → Milestone → Task — so every task can answer “why does this exist?”. Gantt views compute the critical path; flowcharts, lists and boards stay in perfect sync because they’re the same data.

  • One tree from life goals down to 20-minute tasks
  • Critical path & time-left, computed for you
  • Flowchart, Gantt, kanban, list — same truth

Boards & My Day

The day is the unit of work.

The kanban board runs by date, not status — drag a task onto the day it belongs to and it shows up in that day’s worklist. My Day opens with what’s available, what’s blocked and what’s done, plus a one-tap timer so tracked time lands on the right task.

  • Kanban by date — today is always one column
  • My Day: available / blocked / done, auto-sorted
  • Global timer — clock in from any screen

Agenda

Drag intentions onto real hours.

The scheduler proposes conflict-free blocks around your anchors and deadlines — you commit with one tap, or drag tasks straight onto the week. Committed, anchored and suggested time each look different, so you always know what’s a promise and what’s a plan.

  • Earliest-fit scheduling with window hints
  • Deadline & dependency aware
  • Habits get standing slots automatically

Habits & journal

Show up daily — and remember why.

Habits and skills run as a parallel track: streaks, daily-target rings and practice hours that level up. The journal keeps a note per day with live checkboxes, and exports as plain Markdown files — your history stays readable forever.

  • Six streak modes, daily-target rings
  • Practice hours roll up into skill levels
  • Day notes export as plain .md

Automations

The busywork runs itself —
and shows its work.

Rules spawn follow-ups, reschedule what slipped, and keep recurring work flowing. Nothing happens silently: every automated change carries provenance and lands in a review inbox, so the robots stay accountable to you.

  • Trigger → condition → action rules
  • Every output answers “why does this exist?”
  • Conflicts & rejections land in one inbox
