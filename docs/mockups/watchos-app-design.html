Prisms

watchOS — 20 directions

Twenty takes on a deliberately small watch app. Every one of them does exactly two jobs: show today's itinerary and add a task. Same day, same six items in all twenty, so the differences you see are design differences and nothing else. Each direction is shown as a pair — the itinerary screen and its add-task screen. Frames are 46 mm Apple Watch (208 × 248 pt) drawn at 1.5×; colours are the Prisms tones from apps/mobile/src/ui.tsx, lifted for OLED black.

In scope

  • Today's scheduled items
  • Time · title · project colour
  • Done / now / upcoming
  • Tap to complete
  • Add a task
  • Optional project + duration

Deliberately left on the phone

  • Clock-in / timers
  • Focus review
  • Journal & day notes
  • Day-map drag
  • Automations

03

Timeline Rail

Gaps are visible

A spine down the left with a pip per item and a red now-line cutting across it — the same device the phone's day map uses, rotated into a list. Because spacing is proportional to time, empty afternoon reads as empty. Adding taps a gap: the new task drops in at that hour already scheduled.

Ring Dial

The whole day, one glance

A 24-hour ring with an arc per block and a hand at the current time — the day as a shape you recognise rather than a list you read. The centre always names what is happening now. Adding means turning the crown to a free wedge, which the ring draws as a dashed gap.

One thing at a time

Six at once

Preset Grid

No typing at all

Accepts that nobody wants to compose text on a watch. Add opens a grid of the tasks you actually repeat — pulled from your recent and habit history — and one tap creates one. Typing is still there, demoted to a bordered tile at the bottom for the genuinely new thing.

Zoom Lens

Detail where you are

One item is expanded and everything else is compressed into 23 px strips — a fisheye over the day. You keep peripheral awareness of the whole schedule while reading the current thing at full size. Tap any strip and the lens moves. Adding expands the lens into a compose box in the gap you chose.

The expressive one

Typographic

Nothing but words

No colour, no cards, no pips. Light 26 px titles over letterspaced times, separated by hairlines — the itinerary as a printed page. Status is carried entirely by weight and opacity: done is faded and struck, now is bold. Adding is a single field and the return key.

19

Ultra Night

Apple Watch Ultra

Built for the Ultra's flat bezel and its programmable Action Button: press the orange button and you are dictating a task — no taps, no navigation. Type is bigger, contrast is harder, and the whole palette collapses to amber-on-black so it survives night mode and direct sun.

20

Smart Stack

Native to watchOS 10+

Stops being an app and becomes widgets in the system Smart Stack — turn the crown from the watch face and Prisms is already there. Three cards: what's next, how the day is filling up, and a quick add. Opening the app proper shows the same stack, so there is only one layout to learn.
