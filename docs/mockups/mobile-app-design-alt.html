Prisms — Mobile ALT CONCEPT

A second, deliberately different answer to the same product. Instead of five feature-tabs, three modes of mind — Today (do), Plan (arrange), Reflect (look back). Warm paper & ink instead of cool gray cards; hairlines instead of boxes; one ember accent reserved for whatever is live right now. Same data layer, same features — a different personality.

My Day + Agenda merge into one chronological feed — the day is the interface.
Suggestions live in the gaps — free time shows as a dashed slot offering a fitting task.
Pull down to capture replaces the FAB — available from anywhere, with smart parse chips.
Done stays in place — finished items ink out in the feed; the day doubles as its own log.
Projects are metro lines — milestones as stops, not horizontal kanban columns.
Review is a triage stack — one decision at a time, not a list to scan.
